## Vision

The goal of **University Platform** is to provide one connected digital environment for students, parents, faculty, university administrators, departments, recruiters, and campus services.

The product direction combines:

- 🎓 University ERP
- 📚 Learning management
- 👨‍🎓 Student portal
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent portal
- 🌐 Campus social network
- 💬 Messaging and official communication
- 🏫 Campus services
- 💼 Careers and recruitment
- 🏛️ Institutional administration

## Planned Product Areas

### Academic
Courses, registration, departments, programs, semesters, timetables, attendance, assignments, exams, results, GPA/CGPA, and academic documents.

### Community & Social
Campus feed, communities, posts, comments, reactions, polls, events, clubs, and notifications.

### Communication
Direct messaging, group conversations, course discussions, department channels, university announcements, support communication, moderation, and reporting.

### Campus Life
Library, hostel, transport, cafeteria, lost & found, emergency contacts, campus services, and events.

### Careers & Collaboration
Internships, jobs, company profiles, recruiter communication, university/company collaboration, and research collaboration.

## Design Principles

- **Multi-role** — experiences for students, parents, faculty, administrators, and partners
- **Multi-tenant** — institutions remain logically separated
- **Mobile-friendly** — Android browsers and mobile applications as well as desktop
- **API-first** — clear backend contracts for clients
- **Secure by default** — authentication, authorization, validation, and auditability
- **Modular** — academic, social, communication, and campus-service modules can evolve independently
